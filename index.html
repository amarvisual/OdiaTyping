Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about learning Odia typing online

Do I need to install Akruti software to type in Odia?
No! Our website converts your regular keyboard to Odia automatically using the Akruti English Phonetic layout directly in your browser. No download or installation of any software is ever required.
How do I type conjunct letters (ଯୁକ୍ତ ଅକ୍ଷର)?
Press the f key as a Halant/joiner between two consonants. Example: k+f+t = କ୍ତ. For Ya-Phala press Shift+Y after a consonant. For Ra-Phala press f+r. Our interactive keyboard shows you exactly which key to press next with a glowing indicator.
Is this useful for OSSC / OPSC government exam typing test?
Absolutely. Most Odisha government typing tests use the Akruti English Phonetic layout, which is exactly what we teach. Our Expert level paragraphs simulate real exam conditions. Practice consistently to reach the required 25-30 WPM with 90%+ accuracy.
Can I use this on mobile phone?
Yes! On mobile, tap the typing box and type English letters on your phone keyboard — they automatically convert to Odia using the Akruti layout. For example, typing 'k' gives କ, 'ka' gives କା. For best learning experience, a laptop or desktop with physical keyboard is recommended.
How long does it take to learn Odia typing?
With 15-30 minutes of daily practice: Week 1-2 = learning key positions (8-12 WPM). Month 1 = comfortable basic typing (15-20 WPM). Month 2-3 = professional speed (30+ WPM). Government exam standard (25-30 WPM) is achievable in 2-3 months of consistent practice.
