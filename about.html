Who We Are

ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଟାଇପିଂ ଶିକ୍ଷା (Odia Typing Shiksha) is a free online platform dedicated to teaching Odia typing to students, government exam aspirants, journalists, content creators and anyone who loves the Odia language. We believe every Odia speaker should be able to express themselves digitally in their mother tongue — quickly, accurately and with pride.

Our platform teaches the Akruti English Phonetic keyboard layout — the most widely used Odia typing standard in Odisha, found in government offices, DTP centres, newspaper printing houses and professional workplaces across the state. No Akruti software installation is needed — our website handles all the conversion in your browser.

Why Odia Typing Matters

Odia is one of India's 22 scheduled languages and one of its 6 classical languages — a rich literary heritage spanning over 1,000 years. Yet in the digital age, typing fluently in Odia remains a skill gap for millions of speakers. The ability to type in Odia is essential for government jobs (OSSC, OPSC), journalism, education, content creation and preserving our language for future generations.

The Akruti English Phonetic layout is the professional standard used across Odisha. Mastering it opens doors to careers in media, government, DTP, and digital content — and lets you communicate in your mother tongue with the same speed and confidence as typing in English.
